SEMA EO Humanitarian Impact Dashboard

Where explosive ordnance contamination intersects with affected population and blocked access, by district

Scope note: built entirely from SEMA's own IMSMA Core data (NTS surveys, EOD taskings, accident records) — this is SEMA's own analysis for advocacy purposes, not an official JIAF, HNO or HNRP output.
  • Affected-population figures use EOD taskings only. NTS survey population figures are excluded: a check against the live data found outlier entries (e.g. a single record claiming 434,080 beneficiaries) inflating district totals well past plausible population size; SEMA is reviewing this with its information management partners.
  • Blocked-access and conflict-evidence figures are self-reported per survey/tasking, not independently verified.
  • The district-priority score is SEMA's own composite, not a JIAF severity phase — see the methodology note under the map.
